Applied Social Scientist | Violence, Safeguarding, and Public Health 

About
======
I am an applied social scientist working at the intersection of violence, safeguarding, and public health. My research focuses on childhood exposure to domestic violence and abuse, stalking and coercive control, and the development of evidence-informed interventions to prevent harm.

My work combines quantitative and qualitative approaches, including analysis of large-scale survey datasets, mixed-methods evaluation of interventions, and qualitative research with practitioners and policymakers. Much of my research focuses on understanding how systems can respond more effectively to violence and vulnerability, particularly for children and young people.

I have worked across academic, policy, and applied research contexts, including research using the Violence Against Children Surveys (VACS) across low- and middle-income countries and evaluations of multi-agency responses to stalking and domestic abuse in the UK.

My broader research interests include:

- childhood exposure to domestic violence and abuse
- stalking and coercive control
- adverse childhood experiences and intergenerational transmission of violence
- violence prevention and safeguarding systems
- mixed-methods and applied social research
- global health and violence research
