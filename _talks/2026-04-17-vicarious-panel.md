[Trauma and the Impact of Research on Emotionally Challenging Subjects](https://www.eventbrite.com/e/trauma-and-the-impact-of-research-on-emotionally-challenging-subjects-tickets-1982343302180)
This event emerged from growing momentum, shared conversations, and widespread recognition of a significant gap: the emotional and psychological impacts experienced by researchers working with sensitive, traumatic, or ethically complex material through quantitative methods. While vicarious trauma and researcher wellbeing are increasingly recognised in qualitative contexts, there has been far less space to discuss these issues for those working with numbers, surveys, administrative datasets, or large-scale statistical analyses - especially in areas such as suicide, violence, abuse, conflict, and inequality. This symposium was shaped by enthusiastic calls for dialogue, solidarity, and practical guidance across multiple institutions, cohorts, and methodological approaches. I was delighted to have been invited to form part of the panel discussion.
